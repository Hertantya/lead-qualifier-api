# Fun Fact

Fun fact: The word "robot" comes from the Czech word robota, meaning "forced labor" — coined by Karel Čapek in his 1920 play R.U.R.

A bolt of lightning is about 5x hotter than the surface of the Sun (~30,000 K).

Honey never spoils — sealed pots in Egyptian tombs are still edible after 3,000+ years.

Octopuses have three hearts, and two of them stop beating when they swim.
