# Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked.
After 30 days, refunds are issued on a pro-rated basis for the remaining months of an
annual contract.

Enterprise contracts with a signed Master Service Agreement follow the cancellation
terms in that agreement instead of this default policy.

To request a refund, email billing@example.com with your account ID and the reason
for cancellation. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original
payment method.
